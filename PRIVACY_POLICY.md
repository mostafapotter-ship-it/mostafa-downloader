# Privacy Policy — SC Downloader
Last updated: November 2026 · Developer: Mostafa Mahmoud · Contact: Sc.downloder@gmail.com

SC Downloader ("the app") does not have its own servers, accounts, ads or analytics. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any personal data.

## What stays on your device
- Download history, queue, statistics, saved transcripts and settings.
- Downloaded files, saved in the folder you choose.
- If you choose to sign in to Facebook, Instagram or Snapchat (to download stories), you sign in on the platform's official page inside the app. The app never sees or stores your password; the platform's session cookies are kept only on your device and can be removed with "Clear data".
- Your optional Groq API key, stored on your device only.

## Network connections the app makes
- The platforms you download from (the links you paste).
- GitHub: to check for app updates and to update the download engine (yt-dlp).
- Groq (api.groq.com), only if you use transcript/summary/translation: the audio or text you choose is sent to Groq using your own key. Groq's policy applies to that data.
- Google Translate (translate.googleapis.com), only when you use subtitle translation: the subtitle text is sent for translation.

## Permissions
Internet and network state (downloads); notifications (progress and results); foreground service and wake lock (keep downloads running); install packages (apply app updates); battery optimization request and boot completed (keep update checks and background work reliable); storage on Android 9 and below (save files).

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

## Your content
You are responsible for downloading only content you own or have permission to download, and for following each platform's terms.

## Changes and contact
We may update this policy; the date above will change. Questions: Sc.downloder@gmail.com
